# Centre Street Japanese HotPot Six-Month Competitive Growth Design

Date: 2026-08-15

Planning horizon: 2026-08-15 to 2027-02-15

Business: Centre Street Japanese HotPot / 鼎鑽火鍋

Timezone: America/Edmonton

Status: Owner-approved strategic direction; implementation plan pending owner review of this document

## Objective

Protect and grow Centre Street Japanese HotPot before and after the expected Calgary Haidilao opening without entering a price war.

The business will own a clear local position:

> Japanese and Taiwanese individual hot pot with $28.99 AYCE, soup base included, unlimited AAA beef, Taiwanese snacks, milk tea, and AYCE available during regular dinner hours.

The six-month program must:

1. Increase Monday-Thursday traffic from approximately 17 guests per day to at least 25 guests per day.
2. Protect Friday-Sunday traffic at approximately 50 guests per day or better.
3. Keep average spend at approximately CA$30 per guest or better.
4. Increase perceived value through product clarity, quality, service, snacks, and milk tea rather than a lower AYCE price.
5. Build a measured response to Haidilao based on its verified Calgary menu, price, hours, and restrictions, not rumours.

## Baseline and Planning Assumptions

The owner provided the following August 2026 baseline:

- Monday-Thursday revenue: approximately CA$500 per day.
- Friday-Sunday revenue: approximately CA$1,500 per day.
- Average spend: approximately CA$30 per guest.

This produces the following planning baseline:

| Metric | Baseline |
| --- | ---: |
| Monday-Thursday guests | Approximately 17 per day |
| Friday-Sunday guests | Approximately 50 per day |
| Weekly guests | Approximately 217 |
| Weekly revenue | Approximately CA$6,500 |
| Monthly guests at 4.33 weeks | Approximately 938 |
| Monthly revenue at 4.33 weeks | Approximately CA$28,100 |
| Weekday share of weekly revenue | Approximately 31% |
| Weekend share of weekly revenue | Approximately 69% |

The expected November 2026 Calgary Haidilao opening date, final location, menu, AYCE availability, and prices remain unverified until they appear through official store information or a verified Calgary menu.

## Chosen Strategy

Three approaches were considered:

1. **Value and differentiation moat**: keep $28.99, communicate inclusions clearly, and own individual-pot dinner AYCE.
2. **Experience competition**: attempt to reproduce chain-style entertainment and high-touch service.
3. **Promotion defence**: use immediate discounts or free products to protect traffic.

The approved approach combines the first approach with practical service improvements. The third approach remains a controlled fallback. The restaurant will not attempt to copy Haidilao's entertainment model and will not lower the $28.99 AYCE price during the initial plan.

## Positioning and Message Hierarchy

Every major acquisition surface must present the same four primary proof points:

1. `$28.99 AYCE Hot Pot`
2. `Soup Base Included`
3. `Individual Hot Pot`
4. `Unlimited AAA Beef`

The secondary value message is:

> Add Unlimited Taiwanese Snacks for $3.99 per person.

The supporting differentiation is:

- Japanese and Taiwanese flavours.
- Each guest chooses a personal broth and spice level.
- Taiwanese fried chicken, takoyaki, crispy chicken cutlet, golden fried buns, and crispy squid legs.
- Milk tea and specialty drinks.
- AYCE during normal dinner hours.

The $19.99 personal hot pot, Taiwanese beef noodle soup, rice meals, and drinks remain available and visible on the website and menu. They must not displace AYCE as the main paid-acquisition message during this competitive period.

## Offer Architecture

| Level | Offer | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry | $19.99 personal hot pot | Solo diners, lighter eaters, and non-AYCE demand |
| Hero | $28.99 AYCE hot pot | Primary traffic and group-dining offer |
| Upgrade | +$3.99 unlimited Taiwanese snacks | Perceived value and average-order-value growth |
| Add-on | Milk tea and specialty drinks | Margin and meal-completion add-on |

The restaurant must verify that supplier purchasing, menu wording, staff explanations, and actual service consistently support any `AAA Beef` claim.

## Phase 1: First Seven Days

### Goal

Move Monday-Thursday performance from approximately 17 guests and CA$500 per day to 20-22 guests and CA$600-660 per day without a discount.

### One-Week Experiment

The experiment tests one hypothesis only:

> Clear and repeated communication of the four AYCE proof points will produce at least three additional weekday guests per day.

No price reduction, free drink, major budget increase, campaign reconstruction, or competing offer will be introduced during the test.

### Daily Execution

#### Monday

- Confirm AYCE price, included soup base, meat availability, hours, and snack upgrade.
- Confirm the website, Google Business Profile, Google Ads, and store signage show matching information.
- Brief staff on the four proof points and the one-line snack upgrade script.
- Start the daily operating scorecard.

#### Tuesday

- Publish the main value-proof content at approximately 11:30 AM Calgary time after final publishing approval.
- Use English for Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and TikTok.
- Use Simplified Chinese for Xiaohongshu.
- Show real meat, a personal pot, broth, and the table spread; do not rely only on a text poster.

#### Wednesday

- Publish one short real-food Story showing AAA beef, personal-pot cooking, broth, or refills.
- Provide the neutral Google Review QR request to real customers without incentives or positive-review gating.
- Review the first two complete days of traffic, but make no strategy change.

#### Thursday

- Publish the individual-pot message at approximately 11:30 AM.
- Use an afternoon Story reminder before dinner planning ends.
- Confirm weekend stock, meat quality, and service capacity.

#### Friday

- Publish a short weekend reminder between approximately 3:30 PM and 4:30 PM.
- Confirm the telephone is answered with current hours and AYCE details.
- Confirm the AYCE message is visible from the entrance and on the first menu view.

#### Saturday and Sunday

- Protect service quality rather than use a discount.
- Offer the +$3.99 snack upgrade once per AYCE table.
- Capture authentic food and service footage for the following week.
- Record snack upgrade, drink attachment, feedback, and review requests.

### First-Week Paid Media Rules

- Keep the currently approved total budget unchanged.
- Keep Search and Performance Max focused on the dedicated AYCE landing page.
- Keep Performance Max Final URL Expansion disabled so AYCE traffic does not move to a less relevant page.
- Prioritize ad messages matching the four AYCE proof points.
- Do not bid on Haidilao brand terms during this phase.
- Do not use menu views, page views, directions, or general engagement as the primary bidding conversion.
- Use qualified telephone actions as the primary Google Ads conversion; use menu, AYCE interest, and directions as supporting intent signals.
- Do not react to a single day's clicks, spend, or store traffic.

### First-Week Success Criteria

| Metric | Baseline | Week-one target |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Weekday guests per day | 17 | 20-22 |
| Weekday revenue per day | CA$500 | CA$600-660 |
| Weekend guests per day | 50 | At least 50 |
| Weekend revenue per day | CA$1,500 | At least CA$1,500 |
| Average spend | CA$30 | At least CA$30 |

The experiment continues for a second week if traffic direction improves but the sample is too small for a confident conclusion.

## Phase 2: First 30 Days

### Goals

- Reach 22-25 weekday guests per day.
- Reach CA$660-750 weekday revenue per day.
- Keep weekend traffic at 50 guests per day or better.
- Maintain an average spend of CA$30 or better.
- Establish reliable baselines for snack and drink attachment.

At 25 weekday guests and 50 weekend guests, expected revenue is approximately CA$7,500 per week or CA$32,500 per month before seasonal variation.

### Marketing Cadence

Use three main content releases per week:

| Day | Purpose | Primary content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday 11:30 AM | Weekday dinner decision | $28.99 value and included soup base |
| Thursday 11:30 AM | Dinner and weekend planning | Individual pot and unlimited AAA beef |
| Saturday 10:30 AM | Weekend and average spend | Taiwanese snacks and milk tea |

Optional Stories:

- Wednesday dinner: broth, meat, or service proof.
- Friday afternoon: weekend table reminder.

Content mix across four weeks:

- 50% AYCE value proof.
- 20% individual-pot choice and convenience.
- 15% Taiwanese snack upgrade.
- 10% milk tea, beef noodle, or supporting menu items.
- 5% honest customer feedback and local neighbourhood proof.

### Google Business Profile

- Publish one current English update per week.
- Add authentic, restaurant-owned photos weekly.
- Verify hours, menu link, phone number, website, and AYCE wording.
- Request reviews neutrally from real customers through the official link or QR code.
- Reply to reviews conversationally without discounts or promotional pressure.

### Google Ads

Maintain a concentrated structure because the total budget and conversion volume are still limited:

- Search: high-intent AYCE and local hot-pot demand.
- Performance Max: one strong AYCE-focused asset group unless data supports further separation.
- Approximate budget balance: retain the existing roughly two-thirds Performance Max and one-third Search relationship unless 14 complete days show a clear performance difference.

Search intent groups:

1. `ayce hot pot calgary`, `all you can eat hot pot`, and close variants.
2. `hot pot near me`, `hot pot calgary`, and restaurant-intent variants.
3. Chinese high-intent searches such as `卡尔加里火锅`, `火锅自助`, `一人一锅`, and `自助餐厅` where query volume exists.
4. Brand protection for Centre Street Japanese HotPot and common name variants.

Budget increases are permitted only when:

- At least 14 complete days are available after the last material conversion change.
- The campaign is consistently using at least approximately 80% of its approved budget.
- Qualified calls or reconciled store visits support the additional spend.
- Search terms, geography, hours, and landing pages remain relevant.

Any increase is limited to 10-20% at a time and then held long enough to measure.

### In-Store Conversion and Average Spend

- Offer the +$3.99 snack upgrade once, before the AYCE order is finalized.
- Do not list every snack verbally; lead with Taiwanese fried chicken and two other examples.
- Offer one milk tea or drink pairing, not multiple discounts.
- Record the baseline snack and drink attachment rates before setting improvement targets.
- Photograph only restaurant-owned food and obtain permission before using identifiable customer images.

## Phase 3: Months Two and Three

### Goals Before the Expected Haidilao Opening

- Reach 25-27 weekday guests per day.
- Reach CA$750-810 weekday revenue per day.
- Keep weekend performance at 50-55 guests per day.
- Keep average spend at CA$30 or better.
- Build enough authentic reviews and real-food creative that the restaurant looks current, trusted, and operationally complete.

### Operational Moat

The restaurant will not compete through entertainment. It will compete through consistent value and lower friction.

Internal service targets should be measured during the first week and finalized against real kitchen capacity. The intended standards are:

- Prompt greeting and menu explanation.
- Accurate explanation of AYCE inclusions and restrictions.
- Consistent meat quality, portioning, and temperature handling.
- Consistent soup-base recipe and spice level.
- Fast first delivery and refills.
- Clean sauce station, tables, entrance, floor, and washroom.
- Clear response when guests call about availability or group dining.

Failures are logged by category: meat quality, broth, refill delay, missing item, service communication, cleanliness, or payment issue. Repeated failures receive an operating fix before additional advertising spend.

### Review and Reputation Strategy

- Ask all real dine-in tables through a neutral process rather than selectively asking only visibly happy guests.
- Never offer a discount, drink, contest entry, or other benefit for a review.
- Track rating, review count, review recency, and recurring themes weekly.
- Turn repeated negative themes into operating tasks.
- Reuse positive themes in advertising only as truthful paraphrases unless permission exists for direct quotations.

### Pre-Opening Marketing

Four to six weeks before the expected opening:

- Increase the share of AYCE value-proof content.
- Publish real comparisons of what the $28.99 offer includes without naming or attacking a competitor.
- Emphasize normal dinner-hour availability only if it is consistently true.
- Strengthen local Calgary and Centre Street relevance in Search, Google Business Profile, website metadata, and social captions.
- Prepare, but do not activate, the Mon-Thu free-drink fallback.

## Phase 4: Haidilao Opening Monitoring and Response

### Competitive Intelligence Checklist

Verify from official or direct evidence:

- Opening date and exact location.
- Regular business hours.
- Calgary menu and soup-base charges.
- AYCE price, meat inclusions, time limit, party-size rules, and available hours.
- Whether AYCE excludes the 5:00-8:00 PM dinner period.
- Reservations, waitlist, and expected opening-period capacity.
- Opening promotions and their end dates.
- Recurring themes in recent Calgary reviews after opening.

Monitor weekly until official information appears, daily during the 14 days before opening, and daily for the first 30 days after opening.

### Scenario Response Matrix

| Verified Calgary situation | Centre Street response |
| --- | --- |
| No Haidilao AYCE | Keep $28.99; promote price clarity and inclusions |
| AYCE only at lunch or late night | Promote `AYCE Available During Dinner Hours` |
| All-day AYCE priced clearly above $28.99 | Keep price; emphasize soup included, individual pot, AAA beef, and snacks |
| All-day AYCE around $29.99-33.99 | Keep $28.99 initially; consider a limited Mon-Thu drink test only after the traffic trigger is met |
| Heavy opening queues | Promote easy phone availability checks and convenient individual-pot dining without making unverifiable wait-time claims |
| Temporary deep opening discount | Do not copy a temporary price; maintain value messaging and review after the promotion ends |

Competitor names, trademarks, logos, menu images, or advertising copy must not be used in Centre Street creative without a valid reason and permission. No fake reviews, competitor-profile activity, or deceptive comparison claims are allowed.

## Promotion Fallback

### Offer

The reserved fallback is:

> Monday-Thursday: $28.99 AYCE plus one free drink per guest.

This is a limited test, not a permanent menu promise.

### Activation Conditions

Activate only when all conditions are met:

1. Haidilao's relevant Calgary offer is verified.
2. Monday-Thursday revenue is below CA$400 per day, or guests are below 13-14 per day, for two complete consecutive weeks.
3. The decline is not explained by weather, holiday timing, closures, tracking delays, or product availability.
4. Actual drink cost and service capacity have been reviewed.

### Test Design

- Run for 14 days.
- Apply Monday-Thursday only.
- Limit to one approved drink per AYCE guest.
- Define eligible drink size and options before launch.
- Record redemptions, incremental guests, average spend, snack upgrade, and gross-margin effect.

Continue only if the test produces at least three additional weekday guests per day or an equivalent verified revenue improvement without unacceptable margin loss.

## Measurement System

### Daily Store Scorecard

Record:

- Date and day of week.
- Revenue.
- Guest count.
- Table count.
- Average spend per guest.
- AYCE guest count.
- $19.99 personal-hot-pot count.
- +$3.99 snack-upgrade count.
- Drink attachment count.
- New versus returning guest indication when known.
- `How did you hear about us?`: Google, social media, friend/family, passing by, delivery platform, repeat visit, or other.
- Calls that became visits when known.
- Service, stock, or quality issue.

### Weekly Digital Scorecard

Review only complete data periods:

- Google Ads spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, qualified call conversions, and cost per qualified call.
- Search terms, locations, devices, day, and hour.
- GA4 landing sessions, menu interest, AYCE interest, phone clicks, and directions clicks.
- Google Business Profile calls, directions, website clicks, menu clicks, photos, new reviews, and rating changes when available.
- GSC clicks, impressions, CTR, ranking, query intent, and AYCE landing-page performance.
- Social reach, saves, shares, profile visits, and website clicks.

Website browsing and analytics events cannot be reported as actual diners or revenue. Actual store visits and return on ad spend require reconciliation with the store scorecard or POS.

## Decision Rules

- Do not change price, schedule, targeting, bidding, landing page, and creative all in the same test.
- Use at least seven complete days for operational observations and 14 complete days for material advertising decisions.
- Ignore incomplete same-day Google reporting when judging a decline.
- If reach rises but calls and directions do not, improve the offer and CTA.
- If calls rise but store visits do not, inspect phone handling, availability, and hours.
- If store visits rise but average spend falls, strengthen snack and drink attachment rather than traffic discounts.
- If repeated food or service complaints appear, fix operations before increasing advertising.
- If a creative theme wins twice on high-intent actions, repeat the format with new restaurant-owned footage.

### Alert Thresholds

- Weekday warning: below CA$450 or 15 guests per day for two complete weeks.
- Weekday promotion trigger: below CA$400 or 13-14 guests per day for two complete weeks, subject to the fallback conditions.
- Weekend warning: below CA$1,200 or 40 guests per day for two complete weeks.
- Weekly revenue warning: below approximately CA$5,500 for two complete weeks.
- Immediate alert: website or tracking failure, advertising stopped unexpectedly, incorrect price/hours, serious food-quality issue, or a verified competitor announcement requiring a scheduled response.

## Responsibilities

### Owner and Store Team

- Maintain product quality, availability, service, and daily scorecard.
- Confirm prices, offer rules, eligible drinks, and food-cost limits.
- Approve external posts, promotions, advertising changes, and public replies.

### Marketing and Analytics Work

- Prepare weekly content, Google Business Profile updates, and ad assets.
- Monitor Google Ads, GA4, GSC, website health, and Business Profile when accessible.
- Reconcile digital intent with store-provided data.
- Recommend no more than three high-impact changes at each review.

## Approval and Change Control

This design authorizes planning and preparation. It does not by itself authorize:

- A price change.
- A free-drink promotion.
- Publishing social or Google Business Profile content.
- Increasing advertising budgets.
- Using competitor names in advertising.
- Changing the website, Google Ads, GA4, GSC, or Google Business Profile.

Each external publication or material account change requires confirmation of the exact asset, wording, budget, or setting before execution unless the owner provides a clearly bounded authorization for that action.

## Verification

At the end of each phase:

1. Confirm the store scorecard is complete enough to compare like-for-like days.
2. Confirm prices, hours, and inclusions remain consistent across store, website, Google, and ads.
3. Confirm digital tracking is active and does not double-count phone and reservation intent.
4. Compare weekday and weekend traffic separately.
5. Compare revenue and guest count together so a traffic increase is not mistaken for profitable growth.
6. Record the decision: continue, adjust one variable, stop a test, or activate a pre-approved fallback.

## Out of Scope

- A restaurant renovation or new reservation platform.
- Copying Haidilao service entertainment.
- Permanent discounts before actual margin analysis.
- Automatic review replies or review incentives.
- Automatic publishing or advertising mutations without the required approval.
