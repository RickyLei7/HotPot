Quick answer

A first hot pot visit starts with your own soup base

At Centre Street Japanese HotPot, each guest can enjoy a personal pot. Choose from 15 soup bases, then build your meal with meats, vegetables, rice or noodles. It is an easy format for different tastes at the same table.

For a first visit, choose a Hot Pot Set for a straightforward meal or choose AYCE Hot Pot if your table wants an all-you-can-eat option. Add Taiwanese snacks or milk tea when you want more to share.

How does individual hot pot work?

Each guest chooses a personal soup base, then enjoys meats, vegetables, and rice or noodles cooked in the pot. Guests can also add snacks and drinks.

What should I order for my first hot pot visit?

Start with a soup base you enjoy, choose a hot pot set or AYCE Hot Pot, then add meats, vegetables, rice or noodles, and a snack or milk tea if you like.

Does Centre Street Japanese HotPot have all-you-can-eat hot pot?

Yes. AYCE Hot Pot is $28.99 + tax with soup base included. Meat is ordered through the server, and a 19-item snack upgrade is available for +$5.99 per person when everyone at the table upgrades.

Is hot pot suitable for a group or a first-time guest?

Yes. Individual hot pot lets each guest choose their own soup base and meal, which makes it practical for friends, families, groups, and first-time hot pot guests.
