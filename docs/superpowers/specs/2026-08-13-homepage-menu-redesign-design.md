# Homepage Menu-Led Redesign Design

Date: 2026-08-13
Site: Centre Street Japanese HotPot / 鼎鑽火鍋
Routes: `/` and `/zh-hant/`

## Objective

Rebuild the English and Traditional Chinese homepages around the restaurant's strongest current offers instead of presenting a short generic introduction. The page must help a new visitor immediately understand the two main hot pot choices, discover the restaurant's signature Taiwanese dishes, and call to reserve or open the full menu.

The approved homepage order is:

1. $28.99 AYCE hot pot and the +$3.99 all-you-can-eat snack upgrade
2. $19.99 personal hot pot
3. Signature Taiwanese beef noodle soup
4. All signature light meals, each with a food image
5. Milk tea and drinks
6. Visit, directions, and reservation information

## Information Architecture

### Global Header

Keep the existing fixed-on-scroll navigation, logo, language switch, menu links, and telephone reservation action. English and Traditional Chinese routes must remain paired. Navigation remains compact on mobile.

### Section 1: AYCE Hero and Snack Upgrade

Purpose: make the highest-volume group dining offer immediately understandable.

English headline direction:

> $28.99 AYCE Hot Pot in Calgary

Traditional Chinese headline direction:

> 卡加利火鍋自助 $28.99

Required content:

- $28.99 price
- 15 soup bases
- AAA beef, lamb, pork, and chicken
- +$3.99 for 19 all-you-can-eat snacks
- Same-table upgrade rule
- Prominent Signature Taiwanese Fried Chicken image
- Four smaller supporting snack images
- Primary call action and links to AYCE details and the full menu

The existing AYCE poster may remain available as an expandable detail view, but the first screen must communicate the offer without requiring visitors to read the poster.

### Section 2: $19.99 Personal Hot Pot

Purpose: establish the strongest value message for individuals, couples, first-time guests, and people who do not want AYCE.

English headline direction:

> A Complete Personal Hot Pot for $19.99

Traditional Chinese headline direction:

> 個人火鍋 $19.99 一餐完整吃飽

The price must be visually dominant. Four inclusion cards or icons show:

1. Choose from 15 soup bases
2. One large vegetable set
3. One meat
4. One rice or noodle side

The section also includes a compact visual strip for the 15 soup bases and a small split-pot +$2 note. The $24.99 solo and $58.99 couple combos appear below as secondary text.

### Section 3: Signature Taiwanese Beef Noodle Soup

Purpose: create an emotional signature-dish moment after the two hot pot offers.

Required content:

- Large existing beef noodle image
- $16.99 price
- Tender beef, noodles, and rich Taiwanese braised broth
- A shortened version of the existing home-style story
- Link to the full menu or call to reserve

The current English and Chinese beef noodle content must be preserved in meaning. It may be shortened for scanning but must not disappear.

### Section 4: Signature Light Meals

Purpose: show that the restaurant is also suitable for quick meals and guests who do not want hot pot.

Display six image cards:

1. Braised Pork Rice
2. Taiwanese Fried Chicken or Cutlet Rice / Noodle
3. Wonton Soup with Rice / Noodle
4. Unagi Rice Bowl
5. Beef Brisket Rice
6. Sukiyaki Beef Rice

Each card contains a cropped dish image, bilingual-equivalent dish name on its language route, price, and one short description. Images are cropped from the current high-resolution rice-and-noodle menu page with consistent framing. Desktop uses a three-column grid. Mobile uses a readable two-column grid unless the final visual check shows the dish names are too cramped, in which case it falls back to one column.

### Section 5: Milk Tea and Drinks

Purpose: make drinks a visible reason to complete the meal and support the 10% cross-sell.

Use the existing milk tea and drink photography with a concise category list and starting prices. The 10% discount with any hot pot or signature meal is the primary supporting message. Do not reproduce the entire drink menu as dense homepage text; link to the full menu for flavours, ice, sweetness, and toppings.

### Section 6: Visit and Reserve

Keep:

- 2213 Centre St N #2243, Calgary, AB T2E 2T4
- Monday-Friday 17:00-22:30
- Saturday-Sunday 12:00-22:30
- Telephone reservation: (403) 455-3188
- Google Maps directions
- Social profile links and Google review link

The mobile sticky telephone button remains.

## Content and Language Rules

- Build the same six-part structure in English and Traditional Chinese.
- English content must sound natural for Calgary diners, not read as a literal translation.
- Traditional Chinese content must use clear, concise Traditional Chinese.
- Do not remove menu choices to make the page shorter.
- Do not show the Chinese character `稅` in prominent Chinese copy.
- Do not add decorative punctuation to large Chinese display headings.
- Prices must be identical across visible copy, structured data, the English route, and the Traditional Chinese route.
- Avoid describing analytics events, directions, calls, or menu views as confirmed restaurant visits or revenue.

## Visual Direction

- Maintain the existing black, deep red, cream, and gold restaurant identity.
- Use large prices and food photography rather than long generic paragraphs.
- Give AYCE and $19.99 personal hot pot the strongest visual weight.
- Use alternating dark and cream sections to make the long page easy to scan.
- Keep buttons consistent: one primary action per section, with secondary menu/detail links.
- Avoid poster-only communication; posters supplement, but do not replace, accessible HTML content.
- Do not allow horizontal scrolling at 390px or larger viewports.

## Assets

Reuse existing optimized assets where suitable:

- AYCE menu and snack imagery
- Signature Taiwanese Fried Chicken imagery
- Beef noodle imagery
- Milk tea and drink imagery

Create consistent web crops for the six light meals from `public/menu/drink-menu.jpg`. Preserve the original menu file. Create responsive WebP sizes and meaningful alt text for each crop. Do not fabricate or substitute unrelated food photography.

## SEO and Structured Data

- Update homepage titles, descriptions, Restaurant schema, and OfferCatalog to include the current $19.99 personal hot pot.
- Remove stale structured-data language that presents $24.99 as the primary solo hot pot price.
- Keep AYCE, personal hot pot, beef noodle soup, rice/noodle meals, and milk tea terms natural and localized.
- Preserve canonical URLs, bilingual alternates, sitemap coverage, and existing local-business information.

## Analytics and Conversion Actions

Preserve existing tracking and ensure the redesigned actions continue to distinguish:

- AYCE interest
- Personal hot pot interest
- Menu opens
- Telephone clicks
- Directions clicks
- Reservation clicks where applicable

Browsing and menu interest must remain separate from high-intent telephone, reservation, and directions actions.

## Accessibility and Performance

- Every dish image receives useful alt text.
- All text remains HTML text rather than being embedded only in images.
- Buttons and links retain visible keyboard focus.
- Images below the first screen use lazy loading.
- Responsive image sizes should avoid loading full menu-resolution crops on mobile.
- Maintain readable contrast and tap targets.

- Changing Google Ads campaigns in this redesign
- Publishing social posts
